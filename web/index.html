Method & validation

Five sensor artifacts we remove

  1. Resolution. VIIRS 375 m pixels split one fire into many points → counted as unique H3 cells per overpass.
  2. Sensitivity. VIIRS sees fires MODIS cannot → a learned, physically-constrained MODIS detection curve (FRP-binned).
  3. Constellation. 1 → 5 satellites over 26 years → one fixed virtual reference (Terra + Aqua).
  4. Orbit drift. Aqua and Terra drift away from their design overpass times → splice to VIIRS-derived values from 2021.
  5. Non-fire heat. Gas flares, kilns, industry → FIRMS static-source type + persistence mask.

Held-out validation (2017–2020, never seen in training)
